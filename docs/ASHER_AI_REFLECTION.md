# Personal AI Use Reflection

Asher Xu

I used AI to help choose models and understand how they work, including their basic ideas and mechanisms. I also used Codex to apply the models to our dataset. I described the figures I wanted and used Codex to create them and help write the final report.

One advantage was efficiency. AI helped me complete coding, visualization, and reporting work faster than I could have done by myself in the same amount of time. I found its summaries of figures and results clearer. It also helped explain possible reasons for the results and express the analysis using appropriate statistical terms. In this project, I found AI reliable for applying standard models when we provide clear workflow, although the code and outputs still needed checking. Its explanations also helped me understand the models and their results.

One disadvantage was that AI sometimes did extra work that I had not requested, which used additional time and computing resources. Its figures also did not always match what I had in mind. Titles and labels could sound mechanical, be difficult to understand, or contain too much text. I therefore needed to give more specific instructions and review the figures and wording to make them clear and suitable for our presentation.
